## Technical questions to rehearse

**“How do you approach Bronze, Silver and Gold?”**  
Explain that Bronze preserves source data with traceability; Silver applies standardisation, quality rules and canonical definitions; Gold presents curated, business-ready datasets and metrics. Mention that the exact boundaries depend on the platform’s conventions and product needs. Show how you make requirements testable at each stage.

**“How do you profile an unfamiliar dataset?”**  
Describe checking schema and grain, row counts, nulls, uniqueness, valid values, date ranges, referential integrity and relationships. Compare findings with stakeholder assumptions, then document issues, decisions and rules for engineers and testers.

**“How do you define a metric so two teams interpret it consistently?”**  
Capture its business meaning, calculation, grain, dimensions, filters, time period, exclusions, source fields, owner and validation examples. Confirm the definition with the accountable business owner and record it where downstream users can find it.

**“What do you do when the data doesn’t match the business expectation?”**  
Trace the metric back through Gold, Silver and Bronze to the source and transformation rules. Use reconciliation and sample records to identify whether the cause is source quality, a mapping, a business rule or an assumption. Bring evidence and options to the decision owner; don’t quietly change the rule.

**“How technical are you as a BA?”**  
> “I’m comfortable querying and profiling data myself, which helps me test assumptions and have detailed conversations with engineers. My role is to make the requirements and business decisions clear; I work with engineers and modellers on implementation choices rather than prescribing code.”
