Primary purpose of the role 
Lead end to end development of product and platform services through collaborative build, release, 
maintenance and enhancement of product features, to deliver high quality services which provide fit-for
purpose, resilient and reusable services. The role brings together the software development activities and 
drives standard practices that support system-controlled governance and automation throughout. It 
provides technical advice to the team and supports the team’s platforms, maintenance and administration.  
Key accountabilities 
• Drive and ensure the adoption of DevOps practises, continuous improvement, performance metrics, 
providing coaching and professional development initiatives to align resources to deliver high 
quality, efficient and compliant services and a positive customer experience 
• Collaborate with customers and stakeholders to ensure the technological endeavours are aligned 
with the business endeavours 
• Provide technical expertise to product development and platform solution design, and work across 
product teams as part of the discovery process to foster a culture of collaboration and ensure 
optimum service delivery 
• Design through consultation, and implement approved processes (document and train)  
• Build, fine-tune and manage the software development lifecycle framework, applying best practice 
methodologies and industry recognised standards to improve the quality, security and reuse of 
platform products and reduce operational risk 
• Integrate, consolidate, standardize, automate the framework in close collaboration with development 
teams to deliver high quality services  
• Initiate or participate in discovery process with cross-functional product teams to ensure optimal 
service delivery   
• Partner with colleagues and key stakeholders on strategy and vision to develop, establish and 
maintain fit-for-purpose digital services, in order to deliver valuable solutions for customers 
Key challenges 
• Building relationships with the digital team, business representatives and contractors to meet client 
requests and requirements with competing interests, schedules and projects 
• Identifying and balancing competing roadmap, platform services maintenance and project demands 
to ensure deliverables are achieved 
• Keeping up to date with best practice industry standards in a high-volume work environment with 
tight deadlines

Role dimensions 
Decision making 
This role has autonomy and makes decisions that are under their direct control as directed by their 
Manager. It refers to the Manager decisions that require significant change to program outcomes or 
timeframes or are likely to escalate. This role is fully accountable for the delivery of work assignments on 
time and to expectations in terms of quality, deliverable and outcomes. This role submits reports, business 
cases and other forms of written advice with minimal input from the Manager

Key knowledge and experience 
• Extensive demonstrable experience as a Principal DevOps Engineer, or equivalent.  
• Expertise in CI/CD, platform integration, automation and building cloud native solutions.  
• Expertise in standardizing and integrating monitoring and alerting. 
• Expertise in cloud cost management and software development governance. 
Capabilities for the role 
The NSW public sector capability framework describes the capabilities (knowledge, skills and abilities) needed 
to perform a role. There are four main groups of capabilities: personal attributes, relationships, results and 
business enablers, with a fifth people management group of capabilities for roles with managerial 
responsibilities. These groups, combined with capabilities drawn from occupation-specific capability sets 
where relevant, work together to provide an understanding of the capabilities needed for the role. 
The capabilities are separated into focus capabilities and complementary capabilities
